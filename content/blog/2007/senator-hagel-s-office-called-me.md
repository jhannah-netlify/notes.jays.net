Holy crap. Eric from [Senator Hagel](http://hagel.senate.gov/)'s office called me. From the 202 area code. That's Washington D.C. Eric didn't have much to say except that they had received my email (form letter) regarding [HR2826](http://thomas.loc.gov/cgi-bin/bdquery/z?d110:HR02826:) and would take my thoughts into consideration. HR2826 is not up for a vote yet and Eric didn't know how the senator might vote. (... huh? do Senators vote on house resolutions? don't representatives do that? ...)

Still, I'm stunned that they (1) read my email, (2) cared, (3) called me, and (4) I actually got through to Eric and talked to him when I called him back. ... Maybe the system can work? ... Wow ... Stunned.
