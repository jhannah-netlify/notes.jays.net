You have rules. Thousands of them. Good for you. You have exceptions to those rules. Thousands of them. Way to go.  
  
[Ag](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver) is silver. Sure. Why wouldn't it be? And it's electron configuration is \[Kr\] 5s¹ 4d¹⁰, not \[Kr\] 5s² 4d⁹. Of course! Rules are for breaking, right? Absolutely. Hell, you keep good company, silver! Half of Period 5 [breaks the rules](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electron_configuration#Exceptions_in_3d.2C_4d.2C_5d). What's the point in having rules if you're just going to follow them? You incorrigible little imp, you! :)  
  
Chemistry, you're a cheeky monkey and I love you.  
  
P.S. Fuck off.
