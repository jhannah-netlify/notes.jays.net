I just saw a promo of a new TV show about bikers/gangs called "[Sons of Anarchy](http://fxnetworks.com/shows/originals/soa/)." In the ad, a bad-ass lookin' biker dude looks with disdain at the dork in the truck next to his bike at a stop light and says "we're all free men, protected by The Constitution."  
  
Perhaps he doesn't understand the definition of the word "anarchy?" I'm pretty sure *citing legal documents* is the **last** thing an anarchist is supposed to do when arguing his political/ethical world views.
