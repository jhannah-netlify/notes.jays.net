I feel pretty good about my project this weekend. The code I ran is [my fork + branch](http://github.com/jhannah/MSCC-Reads-Processing/tree/qsub) of a bunch of Perl from Harvard against [the latest human genome from UCSC](http://hgdownload.cse.ucsc.edu/downloads.html#human).
