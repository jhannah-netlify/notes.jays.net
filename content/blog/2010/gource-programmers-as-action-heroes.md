Check out [the history of Perl development 1988-present](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jl9HVEJl_-w). As you can see, when you speed it up enough and use lots of colors and movement suddenly programming is exciting to watch! (For a few minutes.) :)  
  
They created that with [Gource (watch other, even prettier videos)](http://code.google.com/p/gource/wiki/Videos).  
  
At \$work\[0\] we have CVS + SVN history back to 2000. It'd be neat to throw Gource against that and see what I (and my coworkers) have been doing with our lives for the last 10 years.
