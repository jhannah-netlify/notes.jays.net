Day 30 of the Paleo “diet.”

Amazing. No pills, powders, meetings, crazy exercise, surgery, money, nothing. Not hungry\[1\]. *Freaking starving* off and on most days was my old “normal.”

I don’t know that everyone’s blood chemistry works the way mine does, but it’s pretty apparent to me (so far) that for my blood chemistry it was not fat that made me fat. It was grains and beans.

17 years of low fat diet, light exercise changed nothing. 30 days of no grains, beans, processed foods have already made a big difference.
