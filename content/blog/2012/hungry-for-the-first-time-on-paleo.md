Huh. Day 15 on the Paleo “diet,” 1,257 calories consumed so far today, and I am *freaking starving* for the first time on this plan. Is it possible a small piece of grandma’s birthday cake and a small scoop of ice cream 2 days ago threw my system out of whack? Or has my body adjusted and my old hunger signals are back? Or…?

Pretty sure the literature says I can cheat every once in a while without the universe imploding…

This right here is how I used to feel *“all the time.”* At the very least I guess I’m lucky that I got a 14.5 day break? :)
