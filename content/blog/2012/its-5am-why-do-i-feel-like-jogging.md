Day 8 (7?) of the Paleo “diet.”

Up at 4am. Weird. I am not a morning person. 40 minutes into my morning routine I realize I’m pacing around the house like a caged animal. I feel like jogging. Jogging? What? I **hate** jogging. I haven’t jogged seriously since high school. What the hell?

Pre-dawn motorcycle ride into work. Full of energy. Maybe I should build a house or something.

Arrive at work. Not hungry. Nuke 2 eggs and an ounce of cheese anyway, since intellectually I know I should probably eat something.

06:10, at my desk, blogging. For a guy that hates mornings, hates jogging, and has been *freaking starving* for 17 years this is already a very strange day.
