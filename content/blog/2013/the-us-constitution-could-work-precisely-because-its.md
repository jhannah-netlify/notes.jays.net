From [Radiolab: Sex, Ducks, and The Founding Feud](http://www.radiolab.org/story/sex-ducks-and-founding-feud/)

> 13:20 \[The US Constitution\] could work. Precisely because it’s unclear. Madison found what he calls a “middle station.” Where everyone can see what they want to see. … The Constitution becomes successful because people don’t agree on what it means. That, according to Joe, was Madison’s epiphony: The Constitution isn’t a set of answers. It’s a framework for argument.

As a computer programmer type, humans and their messy ambiguities in law make my head hurt.
