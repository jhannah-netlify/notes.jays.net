A sad week. After 12.5 years of faithful companionship, Tipper developed some breathing problems. We thought we had them under control for a few weeks. But sadly this past weekend did not go well. We let her go on Tuesday morning. She is deeply missed.

Minnie’s doing great. We walked 5 miles around Chalco yesterday. We both miss Tipper’s relentless scouting, sniffing, and eating of all the things. Including the occasional chunk of asphault.

Rest well Tipper. All our love. \<3
