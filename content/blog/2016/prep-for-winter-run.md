September through December I’ve been living in the Omaha house, with the RV winterized, parked in the driveway.

The plan is to head south for ~10 weeks starting ~January 2. There’s scuba diving to be done in the Florida Keys, I figure. Hopefully lots of dirt biking too.

**Adventure 1**: During the summer run to British Columbia and back, the RV’s fresh water tank bowed within 4" of dragging on the ground. Holy crap! [My dealership in Iowa](http://www.nishnavalleycycle.com/) did a great job taking care of that for me, transporting it all the way back to the factory in Indiana, where the entire tank was re-structured and now looks rock solid for years to come. (Lots of square aluminum tubing reinfocement.)

**Adventure 2**: The unit shipped without winterization plumbing, which surprised me. Two failed attempts to plumb it myself, then my dealership plumbed it for me. (I could barely jam my shoulders and massive pectoral muscles into the work area.) So now I have a 3-way valve, should be easy to winterize myself from now on. :)
