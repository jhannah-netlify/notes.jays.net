## Bimini Bahamas dive 1

2 hour shark dive successful. First tank lasted forever at 30 feet, didn’t need 2nd tank. ~20 hammerheads (WOW. Many huge, a couple really old), ~15 nurse (didn’t bother the feeder too much), ~3 bull (they can be a problem, but they stayed back a ways yay), 0 tiger (can be a big problem, so yay). No dive problems after I settled down a bit. Stupid brain still thinks I’m drowning on entry even though that was my 84th dive. Need a LOT of weight to kneel on the bottom in the current. Went down with 36 lbs (2/3 wetsuit), saftely diver put another 5 on me. Lol. What a day.
